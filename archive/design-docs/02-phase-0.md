# Phase 0: The Cryptographic Dawn (1939-1940)

## Narrative Context
- The player starts in 1939 at the outbreak of WWII, moving into Bletchley Park.
- Transition moves from manual paper-and-pencil methods to the conceptualization of the Bombe.

## Phase 0 Resources
- **FLOPs:** Manual calculations performed by humans.
- **Data:** Deciphered intelligence used as currency.
- **Power (Sustenance):** Coffee and rations for the cryptographers.

## Upgrades & Personnel
- **Junior Calculator:** Auto-generates 0.1 FLOPs/sec.
- **Mathematical Lead:** Multiplies calculation speed by 1.5x.
- **Coffee Rationing:** Reduces Power (Sustenance) decay.
- **Mechanical Hand-Crank:** Increases manual click value.
- **Paper Roll Storage:** Increases Data cap.

## Key Research Tasks
- **Frequency Analysis:** Unlocks the ability to convert FLOPs into Data faster.
- **Rotor Logic Mapping:** Increases Data yield per FLOP processed.
- **The Polish Ciphertext:** One-time Data boost to accelerate early progress.
- **Conceptualize the Bombe:** Final Research task (High Data cost) to unlock Phase 1.

## Bottleneck
- **Human Fatigue:** Scaling too many Junior Calculators increases the Power (Sustenance) requirement exponentially.
