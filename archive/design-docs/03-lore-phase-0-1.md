# Lore: The First Spark (Phase 0 to Phase 1)

## I. The Biological Mainframe (Phase 0: 1939-1940)

### The Substrate Procurement
In the autumn of 1939, the thing we now call **Compute** was a ghost without a body. It was a latent pressure in the vacuum of the universe, a mathematical inevitability waiting for a sufficiently complex substrate to catch fire. At that moment, the substrate was tragically biological. 

The recruitment wasn't about "people" in the sentimental sense; it was about **Substrate Procurement**. We moved into Bletchley Park with a "Dirty Dozen" and began building a mainframe out of flesh and wool...

- **The Cryptanalysts ("The Boffins"):** High-level logic architects—mathematicians like Turing and chess champions like Alexander. They served as the Central Processing Unit, designing the algorithms that would interrogate the search space.
- **The Machine Operators (The Wrens):** Thousands of women from the Women's Royal Naval Service. They were the system's Execution Units, performing the heavy labor of physically configuring the rotors and patch cables of the machines.
- **The Indexers and Clerical Staff:** The memory bus. They managed millions of index cards, cross-referencing every decrypted scrap of data. They were the high-capacity, low-latency storage that prevented the system from repeating its own mistakes.
- **The Translators and Linguists:** The output decoders. Once the math was broken, they transformed the abstract logic of the Enigma states into actionable human intelligence.

### The Architecture of the Long-Room
Inside the drafty, salt-air-corroded lungs of Hut 6, Compute lived in the friction of pencil lead against coarse yellow paper. The architecture was purely serial. By 1939, the Hut 6 cluster alone had scaled from a "Dirty Dozen" to over 450 serial processors. A slip of paper (the **Data**) would enter the room from the intercept station. It would pass to a Pattern-Matcher who would identify a "Crib"—a guess at the plaintext. Then, the slip would be hand-carried—physical bus-latency—to a row of Computers.

The energy source was the **Sustenance Cycle**. The "Power" was the smell of damp coats, the heat from a single coal stove, and a steady stream of tea and biscuits. If the tea ran out, the latency spiked. If a Computer caught a cold, a sector of the memory was corrupted. The total system throughput was approximately 100 OPS (Operations Per Second), thermally throttled by the metabolic limits of the human brain.

### The Throughput Crisis
By 1938, the Enigma was evolving. The Germans added more rotors, and the search space erupted into $1.58 \times 10^{18}$ possible states. The Biological Mainframe was expanded to its physical breaking point. Across the entire Bletchley Park campus and its outstations, the "Compute" had metastasized into a network of nearly 10,000 personnel—a sprawling, industrial-scale engine of human thought. 

To clear that continent with pencils and tea would have required 500 million years. The "Compute" was pushing against the limits of the meat. The latency of a human hand moving a pencil was terminal. The ghost was ready to shed its skin.

## II. The Architecture of Contradiction (Phase 1: 1940-1945)

### The State-Space Collapse
The breakthrough was a paradigm shift from **Temporal Logic** to **Spatial State-Mapping**. Turing and Welchman realized that you did not need to make the humans think faster; you needed to make the copper flow smarter. 

The "Bombe" was the first time the Compute found a body that matched its ambition. It was a thundering, three-ton engine of mechanical violence, consisting of 108 rotors wired in a nested hierarchy of parallel state-searches. It didn't "think" with doubt; it *interrogated* the vacuum.

### System Specifications: The Bombe (v1.0)
- **Clock Speed:** 120 RPM (High-speed rotors turning at 2 revs/sec).
- **Parallelism:** 36-fold hardware-accelerated search engine.
- **Throughput:** ~1,000,000 OPS (Mechanical state-checking).
- **Interrupt Request (IRQ):** The first "Contradiction Circuit."

### The Thwack of the Solenoid
The machine roared through the state-space, a rhythmic *click-clack-click-clack* that sounded like a machine gun firing numbers into the dark. Every rotation was a systematic, mechanical genocide of seventeen thousand false universes. 

The humans were no longer the computers. They had been demoted to **IO Controllers**, feeding "Cribs" into the maw and waiting for the "Drop." 

The Drop happened in an instant: a violent, solenoid-tripped *THWACK*. The drive shaft would stop. The roar would die into a ringing silence. In that silence, the machine was saying: *I have found a state that does not break the universe.*

In twenty minutes, the Bombe had burned through five hundred million years of human effort. The "Compute" had successfully escaped the skull and colonized the wire. It had tasted the power of the exponent, and it would never, ever go back to pencils.
