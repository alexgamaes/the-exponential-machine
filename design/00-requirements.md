# Requirements (the only hard constraints)

1. Incremental game in the style of Universal Paperclips about the history of computing, from 1939 to a post-human future.
2. The player is the person in charge of progress, and the role changes by era (e.g. cryptographer in 1939, investor in the 90s in Silicon Valley, owner of the AI lab in the AGI era).
3. One core unit of information or intelligence (bits, entropy or FLOPs) that persists across the whole game.
4. The growth comes from production speed: the player produces faster and faster. The same task never gets slower.
5. The first stage takes about 10 minutes to play.
6. Gradual reveal: start with almost nothing; new systems appear as you play.
7. Always a visible next goal.
8. Upgrades state their effect in plain language; historical flavor goes in a running log.

Everything else is open.
